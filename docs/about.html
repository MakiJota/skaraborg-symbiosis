About

Hej hej! My name is Jonathan.

This work was done for Skaraborg. It is a proof of concept that uses earlier research as building blocks: a reproducible way to estimate how much industrial waste in a region could replace the raw materials its firms buy. The results are model estimates and may contain errors, so read them as an indication of where to look rather than as measured flows.

Many thanks to Skaraborg and to Leonardo Rosado for the good conversations about the topic.

Building blocks

  2. Patricio, J., Jiménez Encarnación, D., Kalmykova, Y. & Rosado, L. (2021). Top-down method and databases for typical product demands of 103 manufacturing industries. Resources, Conservation and Recycling, 164. doi:10.1016/j.resconrec.2020.105165
  3. Rosado, L., Kalmykova, Y. & Patrício, J. (2016). Urban metabolism profiles. An empirical analysis of the material flow characteristics of three metropolitan areas in Sweden. Journal of Cleaner Production, 126, 206–217. doi:10.1016/j.jclepro.2016.02.139
  4. Evans, S., Benedetti, M. & Holgado Granados, M. (2017). Library of Industrial Symbiosis case studies and linked exchanges (MAESTRI). University of Cambridge. doi:10.17863/CAM.12608
  5. Zhao, L., Sun, Y., Ren, J., Gao, H., Xiao, G. et al. (2025). Construction of waste-to-resource knowledge graph for industrial symbiosis identification using large language models. Nature Communications. doi:10.1038/s41467-025-66599-7
