Regional industrial symbiosis in Skaraborg

Which industrial waste could replace raw materials that other firms buy? Model estimates for Skaraborg's 15 kommuner in 2022 at three nested scales: the region, each kommun and individual firms. One reproducible method, built from official statistics.

Skaraborg – interactive report15 kommuner: waste, imported inputs, material balance and symbiosis flows. English and Swedish. Skaraborg – written reportMethod, results at every scale, sensitivity and validation.

Results are model estimates, not measured flows. Confidential microdata from Statistics Sweden (SCB) are not published: industries with fewer than three organisations in a kommun and imported products with fewer than three importers are merged, and no firm-level locations or links are shown.
